# Duquesne University

**Duquesne University** is the public-facing name used by **Duquesne University of the Holy Spirit**, a Pennsylvania nonprofit corporation under its bylaws. The university identifies itself as a Catholic institution founded by the Congregation of the Holy Spirit (the Spiritans), rather than as a commercial operating entity or a subsidiary of the private companies discussed elsewhere in this knowledge graph.

## Institution and governance boundary

The bylaws define the “Corporation” as Duquesne University of the Holy Spirit, a Pennsylvania nonprofit corporation, and distinguish its Board of Directors from the Corporation’s Members. The institution’s public-facing name, canonical/Spiritan relationship, board governance and nonprofit legal status should not be collapsed into a commercial ownership chain or treated as proof of a particular donor, alumni, employee or corporate affiliation.

The university’s history page says it was founded on 1878-10-01 as Pittsburgh Catholic College by Rev. Joseph Strub and the Congregation of the Holy Ghost. It also records a 1911 name change to Duquesne University of the Holy Ghost. These are institutional-history statements; they do not provide an uninterrupted current corporate-registration record or a substitute for the bylaws’ current legal-name wording.
