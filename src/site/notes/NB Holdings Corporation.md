# NB Holdings Corporation

**NB Holdings Corporation** is a Bank of America Corporation holding-company subsidiary. Bank of America Corporation described it in its 2026 first-quarter filing as a wholly owned holding-company subsidiary; this is a legal-entity and group-structure statement, not a claim that NB Holdings is the group brand, a bank, or the owner of every Bank of America subsidiary.

## Reported ownership and position in the selected structure

The Bank of America corporate-structure page, dated 2026-07-14, places NB Holdings Corporation directly below [[Bank of America Corporation\|Bank of America Corporation]]. In the same selected chart, [[BAC North America Holding Company\|BAC North America Holding Company]] is below NB Holdings, and [[Bank of America, N.A.\|Bank of America, N.A.]] is below BAC North America Holding Company. This establishes the reported path Bank of America Corporation → NB Holdings → BAC North America Holding Company → Bank of America, N.A. for the entities shown.

The chart explicitly presents only selected client-facing or issuing subsidiaries and associated significant holding companies. It is therefore not a complete legal-entity register, capitalization map, asset register, management chart, or proof that every unshown subsidiary lies outside NB Holdings.

## Regulatory disclosure and legal-entity boundary

Bank of America Corporation says that it is separate from its bank and nonbank subsidiaries and has an intercompany arrangement with NB Holdings. The filing says that the parent has transferred, and agreed to transfer, additional parent assets not needed for anticipated near-term expenditures to NB Holdings. The disclosure does not identify an exhaustive asset list, amount, timing, creditor priority, or transfer of every parent asset.

Exhibit 21 to the 2025 Form 10-K lists NB Holdings Corporation with Charlotte, North Carolina as its listed location and Delaware as jurisdiction. That exhibit is a significant-subsidiaries list, so it does not by itself establish formation date, good standing, registered agent, every direct or indirect subsidiary, or current officers.

## Evidence boundary

This page does not infer a direct Bank of America Corporation → Bank of America, N.A. ownership edge, a merger between NB Holdings and any banking entity, or responsibility by NB Holdings for the entire group balance sheet, every contract, regulatory order, or customer product. Those claims need transaction-specific or entity-specific evidence.
