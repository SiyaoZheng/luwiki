# Bank of America Corporation

**Bank of America Corporation** (BAC) is the listed parent holding company of a financial-services group. It is a Delaware corporation and a separate legal entity from its bank and nonbank subsidiaries. It is therefore not interchangeable with Bank of America, N.A. (BANA), the national-bank subsidiary, or with the marketing name “Bank of America.”

## Parent role and selected holding chain

The corporation’s structure page, dated 2026-07-14, shows [[NB Holdings Corporation\|NB Holdings Corporation]] as a child holding company. In the selected chart, NB Holdings sits above BAC North America Holding Company, which sits above BANA. This supports the reported indirect path from BAC to BANA; it does not support a direct BAC → BANA holding claim.

The chart says it includes only selected client-facing or issuing subsidiaries and associated significant holding companies. Exhibit 21 likewise omits subsidiaries that are not significant in the aggregate under SEC rules. These sources cannot establish a complete corporate tree, every direct ownership percentage, or the ownership of unlisted entities.

## Consolidated financial reporting

BAC’s 2025 results release reports, in millions of dollars, net income of $30,509 million, total assets of $3,410,394 million, total deposits of $2,018,729 million and total shareholders’ equity of $303,243 million at 2025-12-31. These are **Bank of America Corporation and subsidiaries consolidated** figures. They are not BANA stand-alone financial statements, NB Holdings parent-only figures, or a measure of any individual subsidiary’s assets or profitability.

The 2025 Form 10-K describes BAC as the parent corporation and identifies consolidated reporting as the relevant presentation. Financial or regulatory conclusions about a named subsidiary require that entity’s own filing or a source that expressly supplies a component-level allocation.

## Governance snapshot

The current governance page identifies [[Brian Moynihan\|Brian Moynihan]] as Chair of the Board and Chief Executive Officer of Bank of America Corporation. It says he has been CEO since January 2010 and Board Chair since October 2014; it also notes he stopped serving as President in September 2025. This is a parent-company governance snapshot, not a complete management roster for BANA, NB Holdings, or the other subsidiaries.

## Evidence boundary

The evidence here does not treat the group’s marketing name as a legal alias for BAC, imply that BAC directly owns BANA, or assign consolidated financial results, client counts, regulatory actions, contracts or liabilities to a particular subsidiary without entity-specific proof. It also does not infer that every entity in Exhibit 21 or the selected structure chart is directly owned by BAC.
